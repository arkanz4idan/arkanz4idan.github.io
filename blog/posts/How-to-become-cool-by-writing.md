Hi and welcome to **how to become a blogger**. But first what is a **Blogger**?

Blogger is a person who writes, manages, and publishes a content from the information, usually bloggers writes about their hobby, personallity, anything that related to a topic, such as subjects, fandom, facts, news, many more.
