Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Void.System. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Void.System website and its available services and features.

Void.System may provide features including user accounts, authentication, website content, user interactions, subscriptions, and other services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Void.System, we may collect or receive the following information:

  • Email address associated with an account.
  • Name or display name associated with an account.
  • Profile picture or avatar associated with an account.
  • User-generated content submitted through the website.
  • Information associated with interactions such as comment likes.
  • Email addresses voluntarily submitted for subscriptions.

3. Google Sign-In

Void.System provides an optional authentication method using Google OAuth.

When you sign in using Google, Void.System may receive basic account information made available by Google, such as your email address, name, and profile picture.

This information is used to create and identify your Void.System account and provide account-related features.

4. Email and Password Accounts

Void.System may allow users to create accounts using an email address and password.

Authentication is handled through our authentication provider. Void.System does not store user passwords directly in the website's source code.

5. User-Generated Content

Some features of Void.System allow users to submit content, such as comments.

Information associated with submitted content may be stored and displayed as necessary to provide those features.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive, confidential, or unnecessary personal information in publicly visible areas.

6. Comments, Likes, and Replies

Void.System may provide features allowing users to comment on content, like comments, and reply to other comments.

Comment-related information may include the content of the comment, the user's display name, profile picture, and information associated with interactions with the comment.

7. Email Subscriptions

Void.System may allow users to subscribe to website updates by voluntarily providing an email address.

Subscription email addresses are stored for the purpose of managing the website's subscription system.

A user does not need to create an account or sign in to subscribe.

8. How We Use Information

Information collected through Void.System may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide website functionality and services.
  • Display user-generated content.
  • Provide comments, likes, and replies.
  • Manage subscriptions.
  • Maintain and improve Void.System.
  • Protect the website and its users from misuse.

9. Third-Party Services

Void.System uses third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These services currently include Google for authentication and Supabase for authentication and database functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Security

Void.System uses reasonable technical measures to help protect information stored and processed through its services.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore avoid submitting sensitive information through publicly accessible areas of the website.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant services, maintain the website, resolve issues, or comply with applicable requirements.

12. Your Choices

You may choose whether to create an account, use optional account features, or subscribe to website updates.

Where available, account and user-content management features may be used to manage or remove information.

13. Children's Privacy

Void.System does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted in a manner that violates applicable requirements, please contact the website administrator.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Void.System's features, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a new "Last updated" date.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by Void.System, please contact the website administrator through the contact information provided by the website.
